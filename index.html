About these calculations & your privacy

The monthly balance is take-home income minus the monthly expenses you enter. Savings coverage equals current emergency savings divided by your entered monthly expenses. These are budgeting estimates, not an eviction prediction, credit score, or financial advice. Daily spending records are displayed separately from the monthly plan; they do not automatically change a saved monthly budget.

Your data is stored using this browser's local storage, not on an account or server. It may disappear if you clear browser data, switch browsers or devices, or use private browsing. Export JSON regularly if you want to preserve your history. Printing may include sensitive financial details.
